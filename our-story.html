    Our story

    Why D1 exists

    A small shop for difficult days, in Bakersfield, California.

    Write to Daniel Visit the shop

    D1 Digitals is a small shop run by me, Daniel Betker, in California. I make grief-support downloads after losing my son.

    Grief is not a problem to solve.

    These resources are here for the ordinary work of carrying it — the paperwork, the remembering, the blank afternoons, the words that will not come.

    with care, Daniel

    I'm a grieving father, not a therapist, doctor, or counselor. These products offer comfort and reflection. They are not medical, mental-health, or professional care, and they don't replace it.

    If you want to write, you can reach Daniel at hello@d1digitals.com.
