    Why D1 exists

    Grief is not a problem to solve.

    These resources are here for the ordinary work of carrying it — the paperwork, the remembering, the blank afternoons, the words that will not come.

    with care, Daniel

    Read a little more

    These pages are for many families

    You do not have to fit one tradition to belong here.

    D1 Digitals is written to feel usable in Mexican and Mexican-American homes, and in Christian, Jewish, Black, secular, and mixed-faith families. Take what fits. Leave what does not.

    If a page uses words that do not meet your family’s way of remembering, you can set it aside. There is no right size for remembrance.

    Questions, answered plainly

    Before you choose.

    Still unsure? That is okay. Email hello@d1digitals.com and we will point you to a fitting place to start.

    Can I get these on the live site?

    Yes. Paid resources on this staging site link directly to Stripe Checkout. The free guides remain on the live Start Here page as well.

    Are these products therapy?

    No. These are self-guided grief-support resources, not therapy, counseling, diagnosis, or emergency care. They are meant to offer structure and a place to put things — not to tell you how to grieve.

    I am supporting someone who is grieving. Which should I choose?

    Start Here is a simple, free place to begin. If you want to offer something more lasting, Memory Well is made to hold memories and stories. There is no perfect gift for grief; your steady presence matters more.
