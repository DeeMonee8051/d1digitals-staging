    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 27 November 2026. This draft is written for the staging site. It needs Daniel’s read-through before any domain swap or publication.

    D1 Digitals LLC is a small shop run by Daniel Betker in California. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what control you have over it. Questions can be sent to hello@d1digitals.com.

    What I collect

    Email list signup. If you choose to leave your email for occasional notes, your email address is required and your name is optional. Signups are stored in MailerLite (the D1 Website Signups group) and used only to send those notes. You can leave the list at any time. Double opt-in should be confirmed in the MailerLite form settings before this policy is treated as final.

    Contact form. Your name (if you give one), email, and whatever you choose to write are sent through the site’s contact form handler so I can reply. Please do not use the form for emergencies or to share information you would not want stored in an email inbox.

    Purchases. When you buy a resource, the checkout collects your name, email, what you bought, and payment status. Payments are processed by Stripe and/or Gumroad. I never see or store your full card number.

    Analytics and basic site data. The site may use analytics or server logs to understand visits, such as pages viewed, browser type, and general device or location information, so the site can be kept in working order. The exact analytics tool should be confirmed before this policy is published.

    Why I collect it

    To deliver your downloads, process payments, answer your messages, and, if you opt in, send occasional emails. That’s all.

    Who helps me

    MailerLite stores email-list signups and sends those notes.

    The contact form is sent through FormSubmit to hello@d1digitals.com. Until Daniel confirms the first FormSubmit activation email, messages may need to be sent by writing to that address directly.

    Stripe and Gumroad process payments.

    Any analytics provider, once confirmed, would receive only the visit information described above.

    These companies handle data only to provide their services, under their own privacy policies.

    What I don’t do

    I don’t sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever. I don’t share personal information for advertising.

    Your choices

    You can ask me to show you, correct, or delete the information I hold about you. Email hello@d1digitals.com and I’ll take care of it promptly. I may need to keep some purchase records for tax and legal reasons. You can also unsubscribe from MailerLite emails using the link in those notes.

    California residents

    If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to ask that it be deleted or corrected, and not to be treated differently for using those rights. To make a request, email hello@d1digitals.com.

    Children

    This site is not meant for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given me information, contact me and I’ll delete it.

    Please be careful with sensitive details

    You don’t need to share health details or emergency information through the contact form. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (US).

    Changes

    If this policy changes, I’ll update the date at the top of this page.
